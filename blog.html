September 8, 2026 · 2 min read

Building my first personal website

This site began as a few separate HTML files. Adding meaningful structure, shared navigation, and a consistent design showed me how small decisions can turn isolated pages into one connected experience.

My biggest takeaway so far is simple: HTML gives content meaning, while CSS shapes how that meaning is presented. Keeping those responsibilities separate makes the site easier to understand and improve.
